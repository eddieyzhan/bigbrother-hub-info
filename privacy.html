BigBrother Mac Hub

Privacy policy

Effective 25 October 2026. BigBrother Mac Hub is operated privately by the person who connects their accounts. It is not a public hosted service.

Access and use

The Gmail connector accesses the authorized account's identity, message content, headers, labels, change history and attachment metadata to maintain a searchable personal record. Gmail requires the full mail OAuth scope for IMAP IDLE. Although that permission also permits sending and permanent deletion, the current Gmail connector performs read operations only. Historical imports and attachment downloads are separate operations.

Connected records support the operator's requested search, recall and assistant tasks. If AI assistance is enabled, relevant records may be sent to the AI provider configured by the operator to perform those tasks. Account credentials are not included in model context. Account data is not sold, used for advertising, or used to develop or train generalized AI or machine-learning models.

Storage and sharing

OAuth grants and retained records are stored in the operator's private server profile on their own computer. Moving the hub or creating backups may copy that profile to another location selected by the operator. Credentials are kept separately from source code and ordinary tool results. The operator controls the server, backups and any clients or AI services they connect.

The connector communicates with Google to authenticate and retrieve mailbox changes. The public information website does not receive Gmail messages or account credentials and contains no application analytics or advertising. Its hosting provider may process ordinary web request data under its own policies.

Control and retention

Disabling an account stops collection while preserving saved records and progress. The operator can revoke Google access in their Google Account's third-party connections settings. Revocation stops future access; it does not erase previously retained local records or backups. The operator manages deletion of those records and backups separately.

Questions about this installation can be directed to the operator using the support email shown on the Google authorization screen.

BigBrother Mac Hub's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Changes to access, use or sharing will be reflected here before the changed behavior is enabled.
